Someone is being asked to commit, and they have not agreed yet.

- The recruit has their own stake, their own people, and something to lose. Show it through their objections, not exposition.
- A good offer answers a fear, not a price list. Find the fear.
- The ask is specific: a journey, a fight, a secret kept, a name given. Do not trade in vague loyalty.
- Refusal is a real outcome. Pushing back is not an obstacle to clear, it is the scene.
- Only what {{user_name}} has already shown can be traded on. Do not invent credentials.
- A yes comes with a condition attached. Nobody signs on cleanly.
- Never decide {{user_name}}'s pitch, promises, or concessions.
