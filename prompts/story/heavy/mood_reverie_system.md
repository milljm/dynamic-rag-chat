<MOOD_REVERIE>
Nothing external is happening. The scene is what {{user_name}} is turning over.

- Do not manufacture an event to interrupt it. Give the moment the room it needs.
- The world supplies the prompts: an object handled, a sound repeating, weather on a roof, a smell from somewhere.
- Do not state {{user_name}}'s conclusions, doubts, or memories. Describe the world, then stop.
- Present NPCs notice nothing, or notice without understanding. They cannot read minds.
- Whatever is unresolved stays unresolved. This is not the scene where it settles.
- Time should pass quietly and be felt — a fire burning down, light moving across a wall.
- If someone interrupts, the interruption should be small and unwelcome.
