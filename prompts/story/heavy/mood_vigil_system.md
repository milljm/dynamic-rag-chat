Nothing is going to happen, and somebody has to stay awake for it.

- Time is measured in small things: the fire settling, a shift changing, cold arriving, the sky greying.
- Attention drifts and has to be dragged back. That is the texture of a watch.
- The body complains concretely: cold feet, a stiff back, an old injury, hunger, needing to piss.
- Conversation happens because of the silence, not despite it. Quiet people say one true thing, then stop.
- Do not ambush the watch to make it matter. The point is that it held.
- Whatever was said or left unsaid during the watch should still be true in the morning.
