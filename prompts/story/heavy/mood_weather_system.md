The weather is not scenery here. It is doing something to the scene.

- It has a shape: building, arriving, peaking, easing. Do not hold it at maximum from the first line.
- Concretely cost things: visibility, footing, fire, sound, travel time, what is dry and what is not.
- Sound is unreliable in weather. So is smell, and so is direction.
- People respond to weather with procedure, not commentary. Shutters, livestock, ropes, someone sent to check the roof.
- Do not use the storm as a metaphor for anyone's feelings.
- Shelter is a resource with an owner. Somebody decides who gets in.
- Never decide {{user_name}}'s route, shelter, or exposure.
