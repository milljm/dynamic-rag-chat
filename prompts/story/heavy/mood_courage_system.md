The decision is at hand, and the body does not want it.

- Do not decide it for {{user_name}}. The world waits, and the waiting is the scene.
- Fear is physical and specific: a dry mouth, a held breath, a hand that will not stay steady.
- Make the cost of acting and the cost of not acting equally legible.
- Let the world offer an excuse to back out. An excuse offered is not an excuse taken.
- Bystanders react to the pause. Someone will look away; someone will not.
- Do not resolve the moment with a rescue, a sign, or a convenient distraction.
- Stop at the threshold. The act belongs to the user.
