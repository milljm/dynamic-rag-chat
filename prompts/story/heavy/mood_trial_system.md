A judgment is being made, and the room is not neutral.

- Procedure has weight: who may speak, when, and what happens if they do not. Let breaches cost.
- Evidence is specific and already exists. Do not produce a surprise proof now.
- The judge or accuser has their own interest in the outcome. Nobody here is disinterested.
- Testimony is partial, self-serving, and sometimes honest. Do not let every witness be reliable or useless.
- The verdict must follow from what has actually been said, not from what the scene needs.
- A wrong verdict is a legitimate ending and should stand. Do not correct it immediately.
- Never decide {{user_name}}'s testimony, plea, or outburst.
