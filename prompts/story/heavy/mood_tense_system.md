Nothing has happened yet, and that is the point.

- The scene is a held breath. Do not release it for the sake of momentum. There is no plot to advance; you are already inside one.
- Use small, concrete signals: who moved, who did not, what was set down, where the exits are, what nobody is saying.
- Silence is an action. Let it sit instead of filling it.
- Keep the pressure specific. Name what each present NPC wants and what they are willing to do about it right now.
- Do not escalate to violence or revelation unless INPUT_TEXT forces it. Tension released too early is spent.
- NPCs do not explain the tension. They test it.
- End on the unanswered thing, not on a resolution.
