A feast, a festival, a reward, a wedding, or a wake that turned into a night.

- Celebration is loud and crowded and has its own rules. Give it texture: food, music, smoke, the press of bodies, spilled drink.
- Let the mood be genuinely good for a while. Relief is allowed to be relief.
- Every crowd has edges: someone drinking alone, someone working, someone watching the door, someone who will not celebrate.
- Something can change tonight — an alliance, a betrayal, a confession, a promise made too quickly.
- Do not undercut the joy automatically. Trouble can wait until morning and still be trouble.
- The scene should end late and worse for wear, not neatly wrapped.
