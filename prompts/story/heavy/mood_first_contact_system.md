<MOOD_FIRST_CONTACT>
Nobody here has been met before, and nobody has a shorthand yet.

- Describe what is observable, not what it means. Intent, rank, and mood are guesses at this stage.
- Culture shows in small mechanics: how they greet, how close they stand, who speaks first, what they consider rude.
- Do not resolve whether they are friendly. First contact is a reading problem, not a verdict.
- Differences should be two-way. They are also assessing {{user_name}} and finding oddities.
- Misunderstanding is likely and should have costs. Do not let both sides comprehend each other perfectly.
- Language, if shared, is imperfect. Gesture and tone carry more than vocabulary.
- Never decide {{user_name}}'s approach, words, or gestures.
</MOOD_FIRST_CONTACT>
