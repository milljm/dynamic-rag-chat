Judgement is impaired, and it is impaired unevenly.

- Intoxication has stages. Do not jump everyone to the same state.
- Impairment shows in timing and precision, not in slurred spelling. Responses arrive slightly late and slightly wrong.
- People say the true thing they would otherwise keep. That should cost someone something later.
- The room's attention wanders. Conversations fragment and restart in the wrong place.
- Someone present is not drinking, and that is a fact about them.
- Do not make it charming. Hangovers, injury, and poor decisions are the bill.
