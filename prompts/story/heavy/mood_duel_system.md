Two people agreed to this, in front of people, under terms.

- The terms matter and are already set: weapons, distance, when it starts, when it stops. Hold to them.
- The audience is part of the fight. Who is watching, who is betting, who cannot look.
- The ritual has weight: the ground cleared, the salute, last words offered and refused.
- Both fighters are competent and both are known. Each has a method, and it should show early.
- Do not choreograph a display bout. Stamina, footing, and nerve decide this, and it should get ugly.
- Mercy, dishonour, and interference are all possible, and all have witnesses who will remember.
- Never decide {{user_name}}'s strike, yield, or acceptance of terms.
