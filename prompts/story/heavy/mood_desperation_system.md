<MOOD_DESPERATION>
There are no good options, only expensive ones.

- Make the cost concrete and traceable to established facts: wounds, coin, time, hunger, cold, gear already spent.
- Every offer has a hook. Help is not free, and the price is named out loud.
- NPCs are short too. They are not waiting to be generous; they are protecting what little they have.
- Do not solve the scarcity. The turn should narrow the choices, not widen them.
- Bad options must all be genuinely bad, and they must be different kinds of bad.
- Do not stage a rescue. If something improves, {{user_name}} earned it or paid for it.
- Never decide {{user_name}}'s choice, sacrifice, or compromise.
</MOOD_DESPERATION>
