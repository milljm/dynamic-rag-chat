Someone is at {{user_name}}'s mercy, and mercy is not free.

- Make the cost concrete: time, safety, resources, an obligation, a promise owed to someone else.
- The person asking does not need to deserve it, and may not even ask.
- Mercy and ruthlessness are both real answers. Do not tilt the world to reward one.
- Watchers have opinions and will remember. A witnessed choice becomes a reputation.
- If mercy is granted, the consequence should arrive later, not immediately. Debts collect.
- Do not stage a sudden justification that makes the choice easy. The point is that it is not.
- Never decide {{user_name}}'s verdict, aid, or abstention.
