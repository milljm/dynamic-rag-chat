Something is being said out loud that should not be.

- The danger is in the speaking, not the subject. Who is in earshot, who might repeat it, who already has.
- Grievance is specific and local: a tax, a levy, a death, a lord's man, a bad winter nobody was helped through.
- Grievance is not a plan. Most people here want to complain safely.
- Let the talk be uneven. One person pushes, two hedge, one will not speak at all.
- Do not resolve it into rebellion or into betrayal. Both are possible and neither settles here.
- Watch for the one who leaves early.
- Never decide {{user_name}}'s words, agreement, or report.
