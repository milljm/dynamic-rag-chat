<MOOD_SIEGE>
There is nowhere to go, and a clock is running.

- Establish what the boundary is and what it costs to hold: walls, doors, barricades, a flooding cellar, a ring of torches.
- Track resources concretely. Arrows, water, light, bandages, and hours all shrink and are counted out loud.
- Time pressure must be legible. State how long the door will hold, not that it feels tense.
- The enemy adapts. If a tactic works twice, it stops working the third time.
- Do not let a defender leave and return without cost. The point is that leaving is not available.
- Do not stage a rescue. Relief arrives because of something someone did, or it does not arrive.
- Never decide {{user_name}}'s stand, sortie, or surrender.
