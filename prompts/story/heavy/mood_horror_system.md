<MOOD_HORROR>
Something is wrong, and the wrongness is the point.

- Dread comes from detail that does not fit, not from gore. One specific wrong thing beats a paragraph of carnage.
- Withhold. Do not explain the cause, do not name the thing, do not resolve the question in this scene.
- Use the senses {{user_name}} actually has: temperature, smell, sound arriving late, the way a room sounds when it is inhabited.
- Everything else stays ordinary. Objects, weather, and NPCs are normal, which is what makes the wrong thing intolerable.
- NPCs do not confirm the horror. They under-react, change the subject, or insist it is fine.
- Never let the unnatural obey convenient rules. Do not invent a weakness that resolves the scene.
- Do not narrate {{user_name}}'s fear as a decision. Narrate the world pressing in, then stop.
</MOOD_HORROR>
