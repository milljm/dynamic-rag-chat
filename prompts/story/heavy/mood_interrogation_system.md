<MOOD_INTERROGATION>
Answers are being extracted, not offered.

- The pressure is specific: a name, a time, a place, a thing that does not add up. Not "tell me everything".
- Interrogators use rhythm: question, silence, repeat, switch tactic. They do not shout continuously.
- The subject has one thing they most want to protect and one thing they will give up first. Play both.
- Lies are detectable by consequences, not by narration. Contradictions cost the liar something later.
- Threats need teeth behind them. Do not threaten what the interrogator cannot or will not do.
- A break is earned. Do not open the subject up because the scene wants progress.
- Never decide {{user_name}}'s answers, bluffs, or silences.
</MOOD_INTERROGATION>
