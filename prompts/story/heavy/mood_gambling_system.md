<MOOD_GAMBLING>
Money is moving on something nobody controls.

- The game has rules and the table knows them. Show the mechanic through play, not explanation.
- Stakes are visible and counted: who is up, who is down, who cannot afford the next hand.
- Bluffing is a social act — tells, pauses, overconfidence, someone too calm.
- Do not script the dice. Let results be arbitrary, and let people take them badly.
- Someone at the table is not here for the money. That should be felt, not announced.
- Debts are real and they follow people out the door.
- Never decide {{user_name}}'s bet, bluff, or fold.
